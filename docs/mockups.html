The app, screen by screen

Every colour, spacing step and type size below comes from mobile/lib/theme.ts, and the wording is what the screens actually render. The design is in the code — what is invented here is only the sample data.

Light is the default. Dark is built separately rather than inverted, and the app follows the system setting, so a phone in dark mode gets the dark palette.

These frames are drawn by hand to carry the annotations. Captures of the running build are in docs/screenshots/, regenerated by mobile/scripts/capture-screenshots.mjs.

DecisionWhy
No coloured header bar It cost 80 points of height and carried no information. Screens open with a large title on the content ground.
Two 40pt figures across a hairline Comparing two numbers is the whole product. It is legible at arm's length, in a dim hall, through shooting glasses.
One slim series bar, one status pill Urgency belongs to the match, not repeated five times over.
52pt buttons, 16pt body text Cold hands, gloves, bad light. Deliberately against the current fashion for thin, low-contrast type.
Inner tens only for pistol ISSF breaks a tied full-ring score on inner tens. Decimal scores break their own ties, so rifle shooters type one number.

Unchanged

The difference between these two screens is not display logic but a row that does not exist in one of them. The database withholds the opponent's report until both have submitted — taking the app apart finds nothing.

What a shooter does after each series, and how the opponent confirms it. This is the step that replaces automated scoring.
